ℹ️ Belgium Capital Gains Tax (2026) - Important Information

  • Tax Rate: 10% on capital gains exceeding €10,000 per year
  • Tax-Free Allowance: The first €10,000 of gains per year are tax-free (indexed annually for inflation)
  • Calculation Base: For assets acquired before 2026, the base cost is the value on December 31, 2025 (last closing price)
  • For Assets After 2026: FIFO method (First In, First Out) applies for calculating gains
  • Non-Deductible: Brokerage fees and stock exchange taxes cannot be deducted from gains
  • Capital Losses: Can only offset gains from the same year and same asset category
  • Carry-Forward: Only €1,000 of unused allowance can be carried forward annually (max 5 years, total €5,000)
  • Effective Date: January 1, 2026
